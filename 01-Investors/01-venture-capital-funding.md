## Common Sources of Funding
* Very early startups may initially raise money from the “Three Fs” (friends, family, and fools)
* Angel investors provide the difference in funding
* As the company grows and needs substantially more capital, it may seek investment from a venture-capital fund

## Center Diagram
* Limited Partners (LPs), such as high-net-worth individuals and institutional investors, commit money to a fund
* General Partners (GPs) manage the fund who select several companies and divide the fund's captial among them

## Power Law
* Because startups are risky, the returns are uneven
* Some companies fail, some return a little money, and a small number grow dramatically

* Limited Partners commit $100 million to the fund
